Privacy Policy

How we protect and handle your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide during the application process, including your name, contact details, financial information, and identification documents. We also collect technical data about your interactions with our website.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used to process membership applications, provide personalized banking services, communicate exclusive offers, and comply with financial regulations. We treat all information with the utmost confidentiality.

3. Data Protection

We employ bank-grade security measures including encryption, secure data centers, and strict access controls. Your financial data is protected with the highest industry standards.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with trusted partners for service delivery, subject to strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. As a member of our elite banking service, you will have a dedicated relationship manager for any privacy concerns.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as necessary to provide our services and meet legal obligations. Upon account closure, data is securely archived or deleted.

7. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact our privacy team. Your dedicated relationship manager is also available to assist.
